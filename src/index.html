THE COKTAIL BAR

GROUND FLOOR

Our welcoming cocktail bar is located on the ground floor, featuring cozy and intimate decor. It includes a fully equipped bar and a small kitchen where we prepare our delicious Dim Sum.

Capacity: Seated: 50 people | Standing: 70 people

We also offer a premium quality sound system and customizable lighting system.

B2B Offer: Ideal for After-work drinks or Networking cocktails near the Amoreiras business district.

THE BASEMENT

The Underground Soul

Two interconnected rooms separated by a secret sliding wall.

  • Room 1: The Mini Banger. Intimate with wall tapestries. Perfect for Birthdays or Private Dinners. (Cap: 25 seated | 50 standing).
  • Room 2: The Acoustic Chamber (Black Box). A sonic masterpiece equipped with a VOID ACOUSTIC system. Perfect for Live Performances, DJ Sets, Film Shoots, and Product Launches. (Cap: 70 seated | 100 standing).

Our center for creativity and innovation, The Badassery, features the Laboratory, reflecting our commitment to quality and sustainability. Circular economy and premium ingredients are fundamental to our approach. We do not offer industrial sodas or added sugars. Open bar service starts at €19 per person.

THE MINI BANGER

BASEMENT ( ROOM 1)

Intimate and Versatile Room

Adorned with vintage wall tapestries, this cozy room offers an elegant yet relaxed atmosphere. It is the premier choice for Private Dinners, Corporate Workshops, and Intimate DJ Sets in the Rato/Amoreiras area.

Capacity: Seated: 25 people | Standing: 50 people

Connection to the Club: The room links to the club through a sliding wall, offering flexibility for larger events or a more intimate setting.

THE ACOUSTIC CHAMBER

BASEMENT (ROOM 2)

Discover our "Black Box", an innovative space with exceptional acoustics. Equipped with a state-of-the-art VOID ACOUSTIC sound system, it is specifically designed for Live Performances, Listening Sessions, Film Shoots, and Brand Activations.

Capacity: Seated: 70 people | Standing: 100 people

Equipped with a brand new VOID ACOUSTIC sound system, calibrated to respond precisely to the unique features of the space, the Black Box ensures an unparalleled auditory experience.
